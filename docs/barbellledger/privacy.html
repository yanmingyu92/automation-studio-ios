BarbellLedger Privacy Policy

BarbellLedger is designed to work without a developer-operated account, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics service.

  • Exercises, routines, workouts, measurements, and training history are stored on your device. The developer does not operate a workout-data server.
  • Training Brief uses a compact summary of recent workout history. On supported devices with Apple Intelligence enabled, narration is generated by Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Other devices use deterministic analysis in the app. Workout data is not sent to a developer or third-party AI server.
  • Imported and exported CSV files are accessed only through files or destinations that you choose. Rest-timer notifications are scheduled locally.
  • No social profile, advertising identifier, health-data sale, or third-party analytics account is created.

Purchases

App Store purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer receives transaction status needed to provide purchased features, not your full payment-card details.

Support data

If you contact support, information that you choose to include in that message is used only to investigate and respond to your request. Do not include private reports, health data, documents, recordings, or API tokens.

Your choices

You can revoke system permissions in iOS Settings, export supported data, and delete local data by deleting the app. Deleting the app can permanently delete local data that has not been exported.

Policy updates

Material changes will be reflected on this page before the corresponding app release.
