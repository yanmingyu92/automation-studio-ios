BarbellLedger Support

Troubleshooting

  • Enable notifications in iOS Settings to receive rest-timer alerts while the app is backgrounded.
  • Use CSV export before deleting the app or replacing your device. Local data is removed when the app is deleted.
  • For FitNotes imports, export the original CSV from FitNotes and select it through the import control without changing its column headers.

Purchases

Use Restore Purchases in the app with the Apple Account used for the original purchase. Subscription management and refunds are handled through your Apple Account.

Contact

Open a GitHub support issue and include the app version, iOS version, and steps to reproduce the problem. Do not include private reports, health data, documents, recordings, passwords, or API tokens.
