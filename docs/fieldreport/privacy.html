FieldReport Privacy Policy

FieldReport is designed to work without a developer-operated account, advertising SDK, or third-party analytics service.

  • Projects, findings, photographs, recordings, transcripts, and generated reports are stored on your device unless you explicitly share them.
  • Speech transcription runs on your device. Downloading or updating a speech model requires a network connection to the model host; this is a model download, not an upload of your recordings.
  • Microphone and camera access is used only when you initiate recording or capture. Exported PDFs leave your device only through a destination that you choose.

Purchases

App Store purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. The developer receives transaction status needed to provide purchased features, not your full payment-card details.

Support data

If you contact support, information that you choose to include in that message is used only to investigate and respond to your request. Do not include private reports, health data, documents, recordings, or API tokens.

Your choices

You can revoke system permissions in iOS Settings, export supported data, and delete local data by deleting the app. Deleting the app can permanently delete local data that has not been exported.

Policy updates

Material changes will be reflected on this page before the corresponding app release.
